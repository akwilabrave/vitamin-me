Install Doppel

  1. Tap Share in Safari's toolbar.
  2. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen , then Add.
  3. Open Doppel from your home screen and connect Strava.

Needs iOS 16.4 or later. Doppel only runs from the home screen, not in a browser tab, so your data stays in one place.

  1. In Chrome, tap the menu ⋮.
  2. Tap Install app (or Add to Home screen).
  3. Open Doppel from your home screen and connect Strava.

Works best in Chrome or Samsung Internet. Doppel only runs as an installed app, not in a browser tab.

Doppel is made for your phone. Open this page on your iPhone or Android phone to install it.

Doppel is in a small, invite-only test while Strava limits new apps to 10 athletes. You'll need a Strava account; Doppel reads your runs, walks and hikes from it.
